## What you're building

Two **custom built-in millwork** bed systems — build, trim, and finish like
site-built cabinetry, **not** free-standing metal beds.

- **Plan Set 1 — Boys Gamer Room Triple Bunk** (sheets A1.0–V1.0): two stacked
  built-in beds + a third lower/floor-level bed, a cubby nook with roof-slat
  detail, integrated shelves, LED + gaming mood lighting, and a built-in box
  stair (with drawers) for access.
- **Plan Set 2 — Window Nook Bed** (sheets B1.0–N5.0): a low platform bed with
  **no drawers underneath**, flanking bookshelves, upholstered cushions, reading
  sconces, and under-platform LED.

## Where each built-in goes

- **Triple bunk:** along the **tall gray wall (the one with the outlet)**. It
  sits to the **right of the door** when you face the door from inside (left as
  you walk in). **Do not build any sleeping surface under the sloped ceiling** —
  the upper bunk must be under the flat ceiling.
- **Window nook:** inside the **6 ft × 5 ft (72" × 60") nook**, right-side ceiling
  ~75". Bed **low to the ground** and **below the window sill**. Bookcases flank
  both sides.

## Design intent (the look)

- High-end built-in cabinetry: face frames, consistent **¼" reveals**, finished
  end panels scribed to the walls, clean shadow lines.
- Palette: **warm-white** paint-grade millwork; **navy/charcoal** accents inside
  the bunk "boxes"; **white oak / Cali oak** wood accents tone-matched to the
  floor; **brass or matte-black** hardware (owner picks — keep it consistent
  across both rooms).
- Ease/round all reachable edges for kid safety; no raw plywood edges visible.

## Must be FIELD VERIFIED before cutting

Every dimension on the drawings is preliminary (marked `[VERIFY]`). Confirm on
site and record on the field-verification sheets (**V1.0** for Set 1, **N5.0**
for Set 2):

**Triple bunk (Set 1):**
- Tall gray wall clear length; room depth
- **Ceiling height at the flat zone** and where the slope begins (keep clear)
- Stud locations and spacing; floor level / high spot
- Existing outlet location; door swing / clearance beside the door
- **All three mattress sizes + thicknesses**; upper-bunk head clearance to ceiling

**Window nook (Set 2):**
- Nook opening width (confirm 72") and depth (confirm 60")
- Ceiling height each side / any slope (build bookcases to the lowest usable height)
- **Window sill height** and operation/egress — mattress top must sit below it
- Clear bed pocket **after** the bookcases are set (frame the platform to that)
- Stud locations; floor level; sconce box locations

## Critical safety / code reminders

- **Anchor everything to framing** (studs/blocking) — never to drywall alone.
  Anti-tip anchors on all tall units.
- **Upper bunk guardrails** on all open sides, **guard top ≥ 5" above the
  mattress top**; access opening ≤ 15" wide.
- **No gap over 3½"** anywhere a child could slip through or trap a limb (rails,
  slats, ladder/stair).
- Use **rated structural fasteners** for all load connections — **no drywall
  screws in bed structure**. Glue + screw all decks.
- **Vent the mattress deck** (slats or drilled deck) so it can breathe.
- Confirm guard heights / opening sizes against **local code + current CPSC
  (16 CFR 1213/1513) and ASTM F1427** guidance. Have a local pro/engineer confirm
  the upper-bunk structural connections.

## Owner decisions still needed

1. **Window-nook mattress fit (blocking issue):** a standard twin is 38" × 75",
   but 75" won't fit the 72" opening once bookcases are in. Pick one **before
   framing:** (a) widen the opening to **≥ 76"** if the walls allow, (b) recess
   the mattress into one bookcase, or (c) use a **custom/short mattress (~70")**.
2. **Mattress models/thicknesses** for all three bunk beds and the nook.
3. **Hardware finish:** brass vs. matte black (keep consistent both rooms).
4. **Access for the bunk:** confirm the built-in box stair (preferred) vs. a
   compact ladder if space is tight.
5. Final **paint SKU, oak/stain sample, and cushion fabric** — approve a sample
   board before spraying.

## Material / takeoff note

The material, cut, and fastener lists in the drawings are **preliminary
estimates based on placeholder sizes** (standard 38" × 75" twin assumed). **Re-do
the take-off after the field-verified dimensions and the mattress decision are
locked**, and add ~10–15% waste. Optimize a sheet-goods cut diagram once final
sizes are set; keep visible oak grain consistent.
